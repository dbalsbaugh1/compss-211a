Explore at your own pace

WASD to move. Left/right arrows to turn. R/F to look up or down. Move the mouse to look; Escape releases the cursor for menus. Click the scene to resume. Walk close and click a physical control: the brass lever moves the shutter; the blue contact below it sends a pulse along the cable. The wells have plungers, and the receiver has a listening plate. Touch uses dragging to look and tapping to interact. E or Space also operates the mechanism you aim at. Guided and text play offer labeled buttons for these same controls. Escape closes settings.
