Explore at your own pace

WASD to move. Left/right arrows to turn. R/F to look up or down. Move the mouse to look; Escape releases the cursor for menus. Click the scene to resume. Touch uses dragging. Walk close to a lamp and look at it. E or Space switches it. Q traces its cable. The central receiver and the wells can also be inspected. Escape closes settings. Every action has a labeled button.
